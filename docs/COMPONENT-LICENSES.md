# Component licenses and package contents

Squancher's Squanch-On publishes original addon code under MIT, alongside the
separately licensed material identified in [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md),
including the GPL-2.0-only Horde reticle and SIL OFL 1.1 fonts.
The standalone rewrite supplies its own unit-frame layout, camera, bag window,
damage meter drawer, tooltip presentation and settings window. ElvUI and BenikUI engines and options addons are neither
bundled nor required. Their behavior and existing configuration supplied
references; that does not relicense their implementations or media.

## What is not included

LibSharedMedia is not bundled. Squanch-On registers its fonts and textures with
it only when another addon has already loaded it. Plater and DynamicCam are
separate addons and are not included or required.

The fonts the author's original setup used (Expressway, Homizio, Forced Square,
Harry P) and the original ElvUI/BenikUI textures are not distributed. The
bundled OFL fonts and generated textures take their place, so text shapes are
close to, not identical with, that setup.

The original ElvUI and BenikUI distributions retain their own notices and terms.
This project makes no redistribution claim about those distributions or their
media. See [installation](LOCAL-ASSEMBLY.md).

Native Blizzard paths and atlases, including the ready-check icons, reference
resources already installed with the game. No extracted Blizzard fonts, textures
or sound files are published here.
Gameplay screenshots retain their documented provenance and Blizzard's rights;
the code's MIT license does not relicense game content.

Names identify their respective authors and projects and imply no endorsement.
